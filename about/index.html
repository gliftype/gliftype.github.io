About

Gliftype is an open-source type foundry founded by Sidiq Kamal Nurmawan and Erwin Wirianata, based in Kebumen and Bandung, Indonesia. We wanted a space to just mess around with visual design and have fun. At the moment, we’re just focusing on producing open-source fonts and taking a few custom font orders on the side. Gliftype will always remain a home for open-source typefaces, so if we ever explore commercial stuff later on, we’ll set up a separate sub-brand to handle it. So yeah, everything here is free for your personal or commercial projects. If you have a project using our fonts, please send it over. We’d be thrilled to see it and share it. Thank you so much, love you all!
