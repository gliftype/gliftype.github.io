About

Gliftype is a type foundry project by Sidiq Kamal Nurmawan and Erwin Wirianata, based in Bandung, Indonesia. We set this up as a playground for visual design and just having some fun. As of right now, we’re not looking to turn typefaces into a commercial commodity (and honestly, we hope it stays that way). Because of that, we don't do retail fonts or any of that sales stuff. Everything we make is completely open-source, so feel free to use them for both personal and commercial projects. (Just don't forget to send over your work! We’d love to check it out and share it).
